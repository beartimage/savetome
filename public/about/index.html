About saveto.me

The useful internet should not disappear after you save it.

saveto.me turns scattered browser bookmarks into a private library you can organize, search by meaning, and ask questions about.

Our purpose

People save valuable pages every day—research, tools, products, travel ideas, references, and work they want to return to. Traditional bookmarks preserve the address but do little to preserve context. Over time, folders become crowded and useful information becomes difficult to recover.

saveto.me is built around a simple idea: Save anything. Find everything. The product should help people understand and reuse what they saved, without requiring them to reorganize their digital life from scratch.

More than a bookmark list

Preserve

Import existing browser bookmarks and retain their source and nested folder structure.

Understand

Add useful metadata, summaries, tags, and smart organization to saved pages.

Retrieve

Combine exact, full-text, and semantic search so useful pages can be found again.

Reuse

Ask questions across saved sources and return to the original evidence directly.

Product principles

  • Private by default: a personal library should not become public accidentally.
  • User control: automation should be reviewable, reversible, and respectful of the user's original organization.
  • Evidence over invention: search and AI answers should show why a result is relevant and link to supporting sources.
  • Portable data: users should be able to import and export their library in useful formats.
  • Accessible design: the service should remain understandable and usable across screen sizes, input methods, and assistive technologies.

A product in active development

saveto.me is evolving. Features, limits, and availability may change as reliability, search quality, browser integrations, and mobile usability improve. Material changes to privacy or service terms will be reflected in the relevant policies.
