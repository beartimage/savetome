How can we help?

Choose the address that best matches your request. Please do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, or a full bookmark export by email.

Contact channels

Use one address for every request. A clear subject line helps route your message to the right review process.

Product and account support

Questions about import, search, synchronization, browser extensions, or account access.

contact@beartimage.com

Privacy requests

Access, correction, export, deletion, restriction, objection, or other privacy rights.

contact@beartimage.com

Security reports

Report a suspected vulnerability responsibly. Include reproducible steps and potential impact; do not access other users' data.

contact@beartimage.com

Legal and business

Legal notices, intellectual-property concerns, partnerships, and other business matters.

contact@beartimage.com

What to include

  • The email address associated with your saveto.me account, if relevant.
  • A concise description of the issue and the result you expected.
  • Your browser, device type, and approximate time of the problem.
  • Screenshots with personal or confidential information removed.

For security and privacy, we may ask you to verify control of the account before acting on a request.

Response expectations

We aim to acknowledge support and security messages within a reasonable period. Privacy and legal requests are handled within the timeframe required by applicable law. Complex investigations may take longer; when practical, we will provide a status update.

Urgent safety matters: saveto.me is not an emergency service. Contact the appropriate local authority if there is an immediate risk of harm.

Self-service options

Before contacting support, review the FAQ. Signed-in users can export their library, manage privacy mode, check link health, sign out, or permanently delete their account from Settings.
