Sign in to saveto.me

Sync your bookmarks across every device. Your library stays private to your account.

You can keep using saveto.me without an account — your data stays only in this browser until you sign in.

Settings

Manage your bookmarks and quick-capture tools.

Import bookmarks

Import a bookmarks file exported from Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (an .html file), or a saveto.me .json backup. Folders become projects; duplicates are skipped.

Export bookmarks

Download all your links. Use HTML to import into any browser, or JSON for a full saveto.me backup.

Re-classify links

Re-run the on-device tagger over every saved link with the latest rules. Refreshes tags and descriptions, and re-files links stuck in an auto-created folder that no longer fits. Your notes, pinned items, and manually-created folders are left untouched.

Quick-capture bookmarklet

Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar. Click it on any page to save that page to saveto.me in one tap — no extension needed.

Check links

Finds duplicate and invalid URLs instantly. A reachability check is best-effort: browsers block reading cross-site responses (CORS), so only clear failures (bad domain, timeout) are flagged — a link marked "unreachable" may still open fine.

Auto-organize

Suggests a better project for links currently in auto-created folders, based on each link's strongest tag or site. Your manually-created (starred) projects are never touched. Uncheck any you want to leave alone.
